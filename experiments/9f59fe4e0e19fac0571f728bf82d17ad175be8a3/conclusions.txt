Conclusion
----------
Noun-only text prompting is substantially worse than role-specific descriptive prompting. It removes the visual attributes needed to distinguish same-category instances and loses several small or low-resolution objects. Do not retain this variant. Proceed to Qwen-generated geometric grounding boxes while restoring visually specific entity descriptions.

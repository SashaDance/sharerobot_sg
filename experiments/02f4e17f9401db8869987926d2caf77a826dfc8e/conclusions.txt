Experiment: global SAM3-versus-SAM2 selection by sparse Qwen anchor agreement
Source commit: 02f4e17f9401db8869987926d2caf77a826dfc8e
Baseline: 48d934e85ddd8b93a570f647f7f5c103a0dbdd01 on the same scenes
Outcome count: 3 fully successful, 5 partially successful, and 2 unsuccessful.

Method
------
For each entity, both SAM3 text tracking and SAM2 sparse-box tracking are run as
before. Aggregate intersection, overlap, and center agreement with the six Qwen
box anchors select one source for the complete 30-frame track. The other source
repairs only missing frames. The experiment adds no Qwen calls and never uses
episode-specific settings. The fixed validation-derived pilot contains one
scene from each of the ten active datasets.

Automated result
----------------
All stages completed for all ten scenes, including DA3, trajectory generation,
rendering, and validation. The selector chose SAM2 for 28 of 29 entities. That
is not independent evidence of quality: SAM2 is prompted by the same boxes used
to score source agreement. Required-object trajectory coverage improved only
for ASU episode 83 (23/30 to 30/30 valid points); the PLEX and RoboSet failures
were unchanged.

Visual assessment by scene
--------------------------

SAM2 keeps the small red-box track present for all frames, improving trajectory
coverage. The pickup/release interpretation remains ambiguous and there is no
distinct object-valued target.

11 UTokyo xArm / episode 72 — fully successful

The cup, mug, table, robot, and pour timeline remain usable. This is effectively

The requested tube is not clearly observable inside the shelf. Source selection

19 UCSD / episode 1026 — partially successful
The manipulated object remains usable, but SAM2 introduces severe robot-mask

28 VIOLA / episode 112 — partially successful
The bowl transfer is still understandable, but the SAM2-selected robot/target
tracks and alternate-source repairs add conspicuous plate, placemat, and image-

49 Bridge / episode 9130 — fully successful
The oven door and whole oven remain coherent through opening. Results are close

60 PLEX RoboSuite / episode 75 — partially successful
The black block remains visible in only 13/30 frames and disappears during the

62 RoboSet / episode 5990 — unsuccessful
The clip lacks the robot and manipulated towel for most frames. The same 23/30
trajectory points remain invalid; this is a data-eligibility problem.

Decision
--------
Reject global source selection as the default. It improves two hard scenes but
reduces the number of fully successful scenes and produces clear regressions in
UCSD and VIOLA. Anchor agreement is biased toward SAM2 because those anchors are
also SAM2 prompts. Keep SAM3 primary with SAM2 missing-frame repair. A safer
future mask experiment must verify quality with independent evidence or apply a
targeted ambiguity detector instead of globally replacing SAM3.

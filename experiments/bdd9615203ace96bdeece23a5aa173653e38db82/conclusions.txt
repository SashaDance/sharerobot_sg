Experiment: sparse six-anchor Qwen grounding on GPU 1
Source commit: bdd9615203ace96bdeece23a5aa173653e38db82
Comparison: 6a92f2661a2123be9ee0d61ff29257801aac002a (dense-box best pipeline)
Outcome count: 7 fully successful, 1 partially successful, and 2 unsuccessful.

Method
------
This experiment restores the best entity, hybrid SAM3/SAM2, and persistent
event-graph configuration, but replaces dense per-frame Qwen grounding with six
uniform box-or-null anchors per 30-frame window: frames 0, 6, 12, 18, 24, and
29. The six anchors are requested once before entity reflection and once after
reflection. Every non-anchor frame is retained with an explicit null box.

SAM3 still propagates its text-prompted native tracks through every frame. The
sparse boxes select the intended SAM3 instance, while SAM2 uses all six anchors
in one 30-frame temporal window and fills only frames missing from SAM3. Graph
inference, DA3, trajectory generation, rendering, and validation still process
all 30 frames. There are no episode-specific settings.

The strict runner exposed only physical GPU 1. Qwen was loaded once for the
complete entity/grounding batch and once for the complete graph batch, never
once per scene. It provided no TP=2 or GPU-0 fallback.

Efficiency result
-----------------
On the same active 10 scenes, audit records show:

                           Dense 6a92f266    Sparse bdd96152    Reduction
Qwen box request attempts        138                20           85.5%
Qwen box image-frames            600               120           80.0%
Cumulative request time       2381.2 s           420.7 s          82.3%

The dense count includes 18 schema/API retry attempts. The sparse run needed
exactly two successful box requests per scene and no box-request retries. The
measured grounding time fell from 39m 41s to 7m 01s, saving 32m 41s across the
pilot. This comparison isolates the Qwen box stage; DA3 and graph cost are
unchanged.

Automated result
----------------
Entity and mask stages completed successfully for all 10 scenes. The first
graph attempt stopped on an ASU response whose final state did not equal its
own transition replay after three correction attempts. Resuming the unchanged
committed graph stage succeeded for ASU and all other scenes. DA3 produced 30
depth/pose results per scene, and trajectory, render, and validation stages
finished with 10 successes and 0 failures. This transient graph-schema retry is
not attributed to sparse grounding.

Conclusion
----------
Six uniform anchors preserve the dense pipeline's 7/1/2 visual outcome while
reducing audited Qwen box attempts by 85.5% and cumulative box-request time by
82.3%. This configuration supersedes 6a92f266 as the recommended pipeline for
the current pilot. The remaining failures are not caused by box sparsity:
Clamp needs single-instance disambiguation, ASU needs role co-reference, and
RoboSet needs goal-video mismatch handling or a corrected source episode.

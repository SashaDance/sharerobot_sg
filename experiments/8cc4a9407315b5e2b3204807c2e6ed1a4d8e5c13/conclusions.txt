Conclusion
----------
Chunk-local SAM2 is a real improvement over using Qwen boxes only to rank SAM3 text tracks: it recovers missing small-object/support coverage and improves the visual outcome count from 1/6/4 to 5/3/3. It also exposes two separate remaining limits. First, SAM2 masks from coarse boxes are noisier than good SAM3 text masks, especially for tables and articulated robots. Second, SAM2 cannot repair an incorrect Qwen role or box, as seen in FMB and RoboSet.

The next low-risk global experiment is a precision-first hybrid: keep the Qwen-verified SAM3 mask whenever it exists and use chunk-local SAM2 only for missing frames. This should preserve SAM3's cleaner masks while retaining the DLR Pour, VIOLA, Bridge, and other coverage recoveries. Wrong-role cases should remain reported rather than repaired per episode.

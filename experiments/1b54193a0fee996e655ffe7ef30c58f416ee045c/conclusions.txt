Conclusion
----------
SAM2.1 is a clear improvement over SAM3 for geometric box prompts and validates the Qwen-as-grounder design. It restores usable masks in most scenes and improves the semantic outcome from the 1/4/6 text-prompt baseline to 1/6/4, but it does not improve the number of fully successful scenes. The dominant remaining failures are graph action/state reasoning plus a few Qwen role-grounding errors, not a universal mask failure. Stop here before another major change and decide whether to add a global role-validation pass and explicit temporal state/action consistency.

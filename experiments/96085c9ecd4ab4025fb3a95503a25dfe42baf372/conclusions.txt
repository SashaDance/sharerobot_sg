Conclusion
----------
This role-specific formulation is preferable to the unrestricted prompt experiment: it removes the clamp and robot regressions and preserves the PLEX support gain. Relative to baseline, however, the improvement is small. FMB demonstrates that a shape adjective can be necessary for a target, while RoboSet requires better role inference rather than prompt wording.

The dominant remaining failure is graph inference. ASU, Bridge, and PLEX have adequate masks but wrong or unchanged state/action outputs; VIOLA also receives an incorrect final relation. The next justified global experiment is to separate per-frame state inference from temporal action inference, then combine the two validated outputs.

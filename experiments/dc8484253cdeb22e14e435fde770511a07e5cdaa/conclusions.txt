Method
------
For the manipulated object only, SAM3 received four global text candidates: the
original visual prompt, the canonical name, "the individual <object> manipulated
by the robot", and "the <object> that moves with the robot gripper". The existing
all-frame Qwen selector chose among native SAM3 tracks. This tests the object-first
candidate-and-prune idea used by AgentRVOS without episode-specific prompts or
rules. Task specifications, graph predictions, and DA3 outputs were held fixed;
SAM3, the mask-dependent trajectory, rendering, and validation were rerun.

Automated result
----------------
All ten active pilot scenes completed validation. Manipulated-object mask pixels
changed in DLR Pour, DLR EDAN, UCSD, and RoboSet. The other six scenes were
pixel-identical to the baseline. The clamp scene still exposed only one native
SAM3 candidate, so relational wording could not recover a single clamp instance.

Conclusion
----------
The aggregate outcome is unchanged from the active 10-scene baseline: 7/1/2.
The method provides a small improvement in UCSD and a usable alternative in EDAN,
but cannot create the missing single-instance clamp proposal and regresses on
RoboSet. Do not adopt these extra prompts as the default. The next improvement
requires an actual instance-track proposal mechanism or independent temporal
verifier, not more SAM3 text paraphrases.

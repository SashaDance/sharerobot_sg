Experiment: boundary-only mask evidence for Qwen graph reasoning
Source commit: 4c215160375af82cbbe4628bd7f2e137c62c2cdd
Baseline: 48d934e85ddd8b93a570f647f7f5c103a0dbdd01 on the same scenes
Outcome count: 4 fully successful, 4 partially successful, and 2 unsuccessful.

Method
------
The proven SAM3-primary plus SAM2 missing-frame-repair policy is restored. Qwen
receives all 30 frames as before, but graph evidence uses opaque role-colored
mask boundaries and role markers instead of 38% full-mask color fills. This
preserves original RGB when a table, support, or whole parent covers most of an
image. No extra model request or episode-specific rule is introduced.

Automated result
----------------
Every pipeline stage completed for all ten scenes. Initial/final graph states
were unchanged in five scenes and changed in five. Mask and trajectory failure
patterns are unchanged from the baseline, as expected for a graph-only change.

Visual assessment by scene
--------------------------
9 ASU TableTop / episode 83 — partially successful
The graph keeps the object on the table and additionally identifies the final
grasp while it remains supported. This is plausible, but the tiny-object mask
and incomplete lift remain ambiguous.

11 UTokyo xArm / episode 72 — fully successful
Initial and final states match the baseline; the plate transfer and stack remain
correct. Only minor event-boundary timing changes occur.

15 DLR SARA Grid Clamp / episode 17 — partially successful
The boundary input avoids the baseline's unsupported final holding relation and
leaves the clamp on the workbench. This is more conservative and visually
consistent with the short clip, although instance identity remains difficult.

17 DLR SARA Pour / episode 0 — fully successful
The initial/final states and pour interpretation remain correct. Event timing
shifts by only one or two frames.

18 DLR EDAN / episode 5 — unsuccessful
The graph remains coherent with its masks, but the requested tube is not clearly
observable. An overlay change cannot repair this data/grounding failure.

19 UCSD / episode 1026 — partially successful
This is the important regression: the final held red object is incorrectly left
on the initial support. The object is very small, and a boundary alone is less
salient than the previous filled overlay.

28 VIOLA / episode 112 — fully successful
The final bowl-on-plate relation is retained, while the false relation placing
the target plate on the lazy Susan is removed. This is an improvement.

49 Bridge / episode 9130 — fully successful
The graph cleanly changes the oven door from closed to open and retains its
part-of-oven relation without an unnecessary final touching edge. This is an
improvement, especially because the whole-oven mask covers most of the frame.

60 PLEX RoboSuite / episode 75 — partially successful
The final inside-container state remains correct. The 17-frame object-mask gap
and trajectory failure are unchanged.

62 RoboSet / episode 5990 — unsuccessful
The graph becomes more conservative, but the clip still lacks the required
robot/object evidence for most frames. This remains a data-eligibility failure.

Decision
--------
Boundary evidence improves large-mask scenes but is not accepted unchanged
because it regresses the small-object UCSD scene. The next experiment combines
the same strong boundary with a low-opacity fill so small objects remain salient
without obscuring RGB under large masks.

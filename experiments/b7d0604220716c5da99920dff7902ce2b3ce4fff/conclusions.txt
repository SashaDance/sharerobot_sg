Conclusion
----------
The precision-first hybrid improves mask quality over all-SAM2 grounding without sacrificing its useful coverage gains: clean SAM3 masks are retained, while missing cup, support, potato, cylinder, and robot frames are recovered. It does not improve the end-to-end outcome count because the independently generated Qwen graph pass regressed on ASU, EDAN, and PLEX despite fixed visual inputs. This exposes graph-generation instability as a separate bottleneck from segmentation.

The remaining failures are not solved by another simple mask fallback. Clamp requires repeated-instance disambiguation, FMB and RoboSet require better role/entity grounding, and several otherwise usable scenes require a temporally constrained graph verifier. Those changes are a larger design step, so this iteration stops here rather than adding scene-specific corrections.

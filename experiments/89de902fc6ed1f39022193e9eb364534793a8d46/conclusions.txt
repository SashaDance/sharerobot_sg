Method
------
After the initial role proposal and five-frame box tracking, Qwen reviews all 30
frames with role-coloured box overlays. It may globally revise the canonical
entity and SAM text prompt, after which the same five-frame grounder is rerun.
SAM3 remains the primary segmenter and chunk-local SAM2.1 only fills missing
SAM3 masks. This is a lightweight adaptation of Refer-Agent's reason/reflection
idea, not a reproduction of its trained method.

Automated result
----------------

trajectory generation, rendering, and structural validation completed for all
11 scenes. Every stage reported 11 successes and 0 failures. Existing DA3
outputs were reused, and trajectory quality was not part of the visual verdict.

Conclusion
----------
All-frame reflection produces one useful global correction: FMB improves from
unsuccessful to partially successful by recovering the manipulated-object
identity. It does not solve repeated-instance selection in Clamp or the severe
goal/video ambiguity in RoboSet, and it slightly reduces some box coverage
without damaging the SAM3-first masks. The end-to-end count improves from
2/6/3 to 2/7/2, but most remaining partial failures are now caused by the
independently generated framewise graph. The next isolated experiment should
keep these exact masks and replace only relation/action inference with a
persistent event timeline plus visual verification.

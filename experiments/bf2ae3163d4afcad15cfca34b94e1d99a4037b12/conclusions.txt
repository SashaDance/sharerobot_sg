Experiment: light-fill plus boundary mask evidence for Qwen graph reasoning
Source commit: bf2ae3163d4afcad15cfca34b94e1d99a4037b12
Baseline: 48d934e85ddd8b93a570f647f7f5c103a0dbdd01 on the same scenes
Outcome count: 3 fully successful, 4 partially successful, and 3 unsuccessful.

Method
------
The baseline entity and tracking pipeline is unchanged: Qwen generates six
sparse box anchors, SAM3 is primary, and SAM2 repairs missing frames. For graph
reasoning only, entity masks are shown as an 18% color fill plus a strong
two-pixel boundary. The intention is to retain the boundary-only experiment's
visibility under large support masks while restoring salience for tiny objects.

Automated result
----------------
Every stage completed and validated for all ten scenes. DA3 produced finite
depth and poses for every frame. Manipulated-object trajectories contain 30/30
valid points in eight scenes, 11/30 in PLEX episode 75, and 7/30 in RoboSet
episode 5990. These values match the baseline mask behavior because this is a
graph-only experiment.

Visual assessment by scene
--------------------------
9 ASU TableTop / episode 83 — unsuccessful
The goal requests a red box, but the entity pass names and tracks a green bottle
that the robot does not manipulate. The masks are temporally stable but belong
to the wrong object, so the resulting graph and complete-looking trajectory are
not valid task evidence.

11 UTokyo xArm / episode 72 — fully successful
The white plate, red target plate, robot, and support are coherent, and the graph
captures the transfer and final stack. Endpoint relations match both previous
graph-overlay variants.

15 DLR SARA Grid Clamp / episode 17 — partially successful
The repeated clamps make single-instance identity ambiguous. The light fill
restores the baseline's final holding relation, whereas boundary-only remained
conservatively on the support. The short clip does not establish a completed
placement strongly enough to prefer the new result.

17 DLR SARA Pour / episode 0 — fully successful
The small container, mug, support, robot, and pour interval are coherent. The
initial and final relations are unchanged from both earlier variants.

18 DLR EDAN / episode 5 — unsuccessful
The requested tube is not visually established; the tracked blue region does
not provide reliable evidence that the task object was found. Overlay tuning
cannot repair this source/grounding failure.

19 UCSD / episode 1026 — partially successful
The object and robot masks remain usable, but the final graph incorrectly says
the visibly held red object is still on the initial support. The light fill does
not recover the correct baseline holding relation.

28 VIOLA / episode 112 — partially successful
The primary bowl-on-target-plate result is correct. However, the light fill
restores the baseline's false target-on-lazy-Susan relation, which boundary-only
had removed.

49 Bridge / episode 9130 — fully successful
The oven door changes from closed to open and remains part of the whole oven.
The new result retains boundary-only's improvement of removing an unnecessary
final robot-touching-door relation.

60 PLEX RoboSuite / episode 75 — partially successful
The final block-inside-container relation is plausible, but the manipulated
object is absent from the mask track through a long occlusion. Only 11/30
trajectory points are valid.

62 RoboSet / episode 5990 — unsuccessful
The clip shows the relevant robot/object evidence only at the end. Only 7/30
trajectory points are valid, and the requested action cannot be judged from the
available frames. This is a data-eligibility failure.

Decision
--------
Reject this overlay as the default. It preserves the Bridge improvement, but it
does not fix UCSD and regresses VIOLA relative to boundary-only. It also offers
no reliable gain on the repeated Clamp instance. Restore the proven full-mask
baseline for current use and stop graph-overlay tuning here; the next useful
work should address semantic role grounding and role co-reference with evidence
independent of the segmenter.

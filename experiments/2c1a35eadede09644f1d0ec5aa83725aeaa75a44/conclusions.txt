Conclusion
----------
The unrestricted 2-5 word visual-prompt change is not accepted as an improvement. It helps generic support/target prompts in FMB and PLEX and helps the UCSD robot, but it also over-specifies or misidentifies entities in DLR clamp, Bridge, FMB, and RoboSet. Graph generation remains unstable even when masks are good (ASU, Bridge, PLEX), so better masks alone do not resolve state/action inference.

Recommended next global revision
--------------------------------
Use role-specific prompt rules: force the robot SAM prompt to "robot arm"; keep the manipulated object to dominant color plus one common visual noun; allow one discriminative color/material/shape descriptor for supports and targets. Define initial support from the first frames and target from the observed final destination, allowing null when no destination is visible. This preserves the useful specificity without the regressions caused by technical or overly specific phrases.
